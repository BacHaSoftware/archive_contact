Auto Contact Archiver

Auto Contact Archiver by Bac Ha Software is an Odoo add‑on that automatically archives contacts once related processes are completed. By keeping the database clean, it reduces manual effort and ensures effortless management.

Auto Contact Archiver là giải pháp lưu trữ liên hệ tự động trên Odoo ngay khi hoàn thành các tác vụ liên quan. Ứng dụng giúp giải phóng bộ nhớ và giữ cho cơ sở dữ liệu luôn tinh gọn, giúp việc quản trị dữ liệu trở nên dễ dàng.

Linked
Archive Linked Contacts

When a user is archived from the Odoo Users menu, the module automatically archives the related contact. The archived contact is hidden from standard contact lists and only appears in the archived view.

  • Module automatically deactivates related contact cards.
  • Archived personnel data disappears instantly from standard directory views.
  • Users easily locate archived contact records inside specific repositories.
Automated
Archive Applicant Contacts

When an applicant is marked as Refused, the module automatically archives the contact associated with that applicant. The contact is then removed from active contact lists.

  • System instantly flags rejected applicants to trigger automatic archiving processes.
  • Rejected candidate contact sheets automatically vanish from operational lists.
  • Clean contact lists smoothly separate active applicants from refused ones.
Hierarchical
Archive Child Contacts

When a company or parent contact is archived, all child contacts linked to that record are automatically archived as well. Archived contacts are hidden from standard list views.

  • Archiving a parent company automatically process all connected child records.
  • All related child contacts instantly move into archived storage together.
  • Standard lists automatically hide all archived family and company relationships.
